One plan for the whole house

Every room knows what it needs.

Chorus+ draws your home as a simple floor plan. Chores, supplies and spending all live in the room they belong to.

Living room

Today's chores, at a glance

Tap once to mark it done, or finish several at once. What's left shows up room by room.

Balcony

Weather, for housework

When to dry laundry, take an umbrella or air out the house.

Bathroom

Fair turns

Rotating duties, swap requests and a weekly look at who did what.

Kitchen

Supplies before they run out

Running-low lists, expiry dates and a shopping list that flows into your expenses.

Bedroom

A ledger that's yours

Keep personal spending private, right next to the shared budget.

Dining

Settle up, done for you

Who owes whom, and exactly how it adds up.

Entrance

On your Home Screen

Widgets, Live Activities and reminders you choose.

Weather window

Look out the window before you hang the laundry.

Your neighborhood's sky, painted as a window — sunrise, rain, snow or a quiet night. Below it, a few plain notes for the day.

  • The best dry-weather hours for laundry
  • Whether you'll need an umbrella
  • Whether it's a good time to air out the house

Shared money

What's left to spend this month, in one number.

Shared expenses and your own ledger, side by side. Budgets by category, recurring bills, installments and bank statement import.

  • Split an expense and everyone's name is stamped on it
  • Settle up with the math shown, not hidden
  • Record payment alerts instantly with iPhone Shortcuts

Supplies

Never the last roll of paper towels again.

Chorus+ learns how fast you use things and tells you before they run out. Expiry dates come first, and the shopping list is one tap away.

Look back

A month of home life, on one page.

Chores finished, money shared and days recorded — gathered into a calendar you can keep or share as an image.

No ads, no tracking

We don't use analytics or ad tracking. Your home is not a data source.

Private where it should be

Your personal ledger and photos stay hidden from the rest of the household.

Yours to take or delete

Export your records or delete your account from Settings, anytime.

Try the sample home first.

No sign-up needed to look around. When you're ready, invite your household with a code.
