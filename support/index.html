How can we help?

Chorus+ (살림+ in Korea) helps people who live together share chores, supplies, shopping and household money.

Ask us

In the app, open Settings → News & help to send a question. We usually reply within two days, and you can attach photos.

If you can’t open the app, email us: hello@chorusplus.app

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete home or account → Delete account anytime. Your personal ledger, personal photos and support questions are deleted with it.

Export your records

In Money → Money menu → Export to Excel, you can save your ledger as a file.
